## Changes

- **Behaviour:**
  - Shadow outcomes persisted 5 s after the decision (so the door engine's event for the same passage can be paired), classified agree / shadow-only / legacy-only / identity-mismatch / none; identity mismatches logged as "cần kiểm tra".
  - `pipelineContext()` uses `pipelineFusionThresholds(tag)` (same numbers as the door engine for FP32 r50; logged once).
  - Recognised faces of a GRANTED frame stored as observations (employeeId, matchCosine, matchMargin) next to tailgater faces; excluded from stranger grouping.
  - Camera adaptation job every 10 min (first 3 min after start): `planAdaptation` policy (cosine ≥ acceptSingle + 0.10, margin ≥ 0.15, quality ≥ 0.35, 5 per camera, one per access event, novelty 0.05); templates source "adaptation", attributed to the event, deletable; manual template cap ignores them; writes settled before the SSE.
  - Stranger clusters carry `suggestion` (evidence floor 0.35; missing cameras listed); GET templates gains `coverage`.
  - `PIPELINE_MIN_FACE_PX`: worker-only face floor (unset = door engine's).
- **API:** GET /api/pipeline/shadow-summary?hours= (viewer), GET /api/pipeline/shadow-results (viewer, keyset, filters); RangeError → 400. Cluster `suggestion`, templates `coverage`.
- **Schema (acc-db):** pipeline_shadow_results; stranger_faces + employeeId/matchCosine/matchMargin; templates source "adaptation". Additive; rollback needs no DDL (see acc-db handoff for the observation purge caveat).
- **Environment:** SHADOW_RESULT_RETENTION_DAYS (30), PIPELINE_MIN_FACE_PX (unset), FACE_TTA_FLIP (0); compose passthrough.
- **Security/privacy:** no embeddings in any response; shadow results hold numbers only; adaptation cannot create employees or change access; observations share the 14-day stranger-face purge.
